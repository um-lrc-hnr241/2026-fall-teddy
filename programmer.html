Ideas about art and objects

Meet the interior designer

Alfred Gell (1945–1997) was a British social anthropologist who studied art and the ways objects connect people.

Although he was not an interior designer, his ideas help us think about how objects can carry meaning and shape relationships in a space like our playroom.

Art, agency, and objects

In Art and Agency, published after his death in 1998, Gell explored how artworks can act within social relationships. His work gives us another way to think about the objects we make and share.
